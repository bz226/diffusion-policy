One seed per method (seed 0), 80 episodes per point; the shared starting point pools 240 original episodes. Training adds N(0, 0.1²) noise to every action at the last denoising step, so training returns sit below evaluation returns. DPPO retains its ±3σ truncation of this nominal Gaussian noise. The pooled starting-point standard error is unavailable because original episode spreads were not saved.
