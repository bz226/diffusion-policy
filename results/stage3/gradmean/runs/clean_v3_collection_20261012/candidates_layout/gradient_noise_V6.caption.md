The paper's score estimator with frozen critic baseline along the DPPO, without_PPO and without_PPO_and_GAE seed-0 paths; each point: m = 40,000 episodes (500 batches of 80). Panel A uses V4's pairwise signal estimate on the same batches; panels B/C use this estimator's per-episode variance trace and its ratio to that V4 squared signal. Paper units multiply code gradients by 2,500; all panels have log y-axes, and error bars are 90% delete-one-50-batch-group jackknife intervals with t quantiles (9 degrees of freedom), jointly recomputing ratios. Marker offsets are −2, 0 and +2 updates; the pretrained point is drawn once in gray. Open triangles are approved upper-signal plug-in lower bounds when signal is unresolved; these are not two-sided confidence intervals, and missing values are not replaced using larger cohorts. An average over n episodes has relative variance B/n, so one training update (80 episodes) has B/80. Intervals are conditional on the measured checkpoints (one training seed per path). The critic at P0 was warmed for 20 batches and then frozen; it is not the untrained critic that training starts with. At every other point it is that run's own critic, restored from the checkpoint, given 5 warm-up batches and then frozen. Hollow dotted overlays use V1 for DPPO and without_PPO and V2 for without_PPO_and_GAE, relative to each estimator's own mean. Their two distinct pretrained values are listed in the table and omitted from the overlay to keep a single shared origin. These frozen-critic, full-batch-normalized measurements are proxies for training's evolving critic and per-chunk normalization. Pending complete equal-size measurements: C1, C2.
