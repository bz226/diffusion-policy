Round 2 measures the V4 mean discounted-return gradient and its noise at five fixed policies: P0 is pretrained; D1/D2 and N1/N2 are the middle/final DPPO and NC4 seed-0 checkpoints. The x-axis is actor-parameter L2 distance from P0; the logarithmic y-axes show gradient norm, episode-equivalent covariance trace (80 times whole-batch variance), and noise scale B = trace / squared signal in episodes, with V4 gradient moments converted to paper units. Each point uses 1,400 fresh 80-episode batches, grouped into 28 independent groups of 50; bars are the existing 90% delete-one-group jackknife intervals, and lines only connect measured checkpoints. Mean gradients and noise at the later policies are smaller than at P0, while B remains 58,000–94,000 episodes, far above the 80-episode training batch. The estimated mean-direction cosine is only 0.75–0.81, and these conditional single-path measurements do not establish that noise caused a training outcome.
